    https://wz2100.net

"[Year]" is a placeholder for the year of the committer date of the
most recent commit in the version-control history of the source tree
from which the build was produced or, where that is unavailable, the
year recorded for that purpose in the source distribution. A work other
than the original version may instead use the year of the most recent
commit from the official Warzone 2100 source repository that it
includes.

The notice is not translated. "©" may be used in place of "(C)", and
the notice may be preceded by "Portions" or similar words where it

3. SUMMARY FOR DISTRIBUTORS AND FORKS (informational)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This section is a summary only. The binding terms are those in
COPYING.SECTION7; where this summary differs from them, those terms
govern.

- Distribution packages: builds whose changes are limited to selecting
  build options the build system provides, building and packaging,
  system and platform compatibility, launchers and sandboxing wrappers,
  security fixes (disabling features only as far as reasonably
  necessary), bug fixes from upstream, and upstream translations need no
  renaming and no "unofficial modified version" notice. This holds
  provided that, apart from the direct effects of those changes, the
  game is otherwise unchanged; that they remove or disable nothing
  except through those build options or as a security fix requires; that
  they add no data collection, data transmission, or advertising; and
  that the required notices are left intact.

- Modified versions and forks of the game that are distributed: may
  say they are based on Warzone 2100, but must use a product name that
  does not include "Warzone 2100" (or a variant such as "Warzone2100"
  or "WZ2100"); must clearly display a notice identifying them as an
  unofficial modified version of Warzone 2100 on the main menu (or
  somewhere at least as prominent), and in their startup banner or
  version output where the original version prints one; and must
  include the designated copyright notice above in their Appropriate
  Legal Notices, and are asked to include the project website link as
  well. Executable, application bundle, package, file, and directory
  names need not change, though they may. Versions modified only for
  private use, and never distributed, need no marking.

- Not affected by the marking rules: separate programs that use only
  portions of material under these terms and are not likely to be taken
  for the game; mods, maps, and add-ons (unless a distribution installs
  or enables by default mods, other than those published by the
  Warzone 2100 Project, that modify or replace material under these
  terms); and test builds of changes proposed upstream that are labeled
  as such. All of these must still not misrepresent their origin, and
  those that display Appropriate Legal Notices must include the
  designated copyright notice in them.

- Build provenance: builds record information about their origin, such
  as the DISTRIBUTOR value, version-control details (commit, branch,
  tag), and build manifest publisher. Under clause 2(a) of
  COPYING.SECTION7, only builds produced by the Warzone 2100 Project
  (including builds made on its behalf) may use the DISTRIBUTOR value
  "wz2100.net" or carry provenance information copied from an official
  build, and provenance information may not be set or altered so that
  another build appears to come from the Warzone 2100 Project.
  Information a build generates itself, from the source and data it was
  actually built from or from its own outputs (such as file hashes, or
  the commit recorded in a source archive it was built from), is not
  copied, even where it matches an official build's.
